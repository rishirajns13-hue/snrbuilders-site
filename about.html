Built by the engineers who design it

A home is not just a transaction. It is a structure meant to shelter a family for three generations.

SNR Builders was founded to close the gap between real-estate marketing and structural engineering. Our founders are practising civil engineers. They are on the slab checking steel and slump tests, not handing the work to sub-contractors.

Buy a flat from us and you deal with the people who designed and poured it.

What we check, in order

  1. Soil testing

    Borehole and bearing-capacity tests decide the foundation design.

  2. RCC frame

    High-yield TMT steel and design-mix concrete, with a slump test on every pour.

  3. Masonry

    Wire-cut brick or solid cement block for dense, crack-resistant walls.

  4. Waterproofing

    Membrane on bathrooms, balconies and terrace, then a 72-hour pond test.

  5. Doors and joinery

    Frames, doors and fittings checked as they go in.

  6. Handover audit

    Electrical, plumbing and lift checks before possession.
